    How to Play

    📖 Instructions

    Click the Network! button to earn connections. Use connections to buy upgrades that increase your earnings per click or per second.

    • Connections: Your main resource earned by clicking or through upgrades
    • Per Click: The amount of connections you get from each click
    • Per Second: The amount of connections you earn automatically each second
    • Upgrades: Improvements that increase your earnings
    • Rewards: Achievements you unlock by reaching milestones
